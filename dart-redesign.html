Our story

"We built DART because division leaders and EOCs needed accurate, real-time data and had no practical way to collect, analyze, or report it."

DART was created from direct experience in disaster environments. The platform was developed while supporting long-term disaster operations. What began as a simple way to track survivors, shelters, and assets grew into a platform for shelter management, situational reporting, volunteer coordination, and debris operations.

Every DART product is shaped by lessons learned in the field. Not theory, not buzzwords, and not technology for technology's sake.

Questions emergency managers ask us

Straight answers.

How fast can we be up and running?

Most single-product deployments are live within a few weeks, including training. During an active incident we prioritize activation support so you can stand up quickly.

Does it work in the field with poor connectivity?

DART is designed for field use. Ask us about the specific offline and low-bandwidth behavior for the product you need, so you know exactly what to expect before an activation.

Who owns the data?

You do. Export your data at any time in standard formats. We never sell or share agency or client data.

Is client and survivor data protected?

Shelter and client records are sensitive. DART uses role-based access and keeps an audit trail of who viewed and changed records. Request our security overview for the details your IT team will want.

Can we start with one product and add more later?

Yes. Many agencies start with the IAP Builder or Shelter Management and add Debris and Substantial Damage as needs change. Your existing data carries over.

What does support look like during an activation?

You reach a person who understands ICS and operational periods, by phone. Priority support is included on Bundle and State plans.
